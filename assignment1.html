Welcome to Government Polytechnic Aadampur, Tarabganj, Gonda. This Government Polytechnic is established in 2013 with the assistance of central government with the aim of developing rural area and inculcating technical skill in the youth to accept global challenges. Three-year Diploma course in Mechanical Engineering (Production) started in year 2013. Mechanical Engineering (Automobile) and Computer Science & Engineering started in year 2015.
